Introduction

Tinker's Toys

Tinker’s Toys is a children’s toy company that focuses on creating high-quality, wooden toys, hand-crafted by professional carpenters for your child’s enjoyment. We specialize in rocking horses, building blocks, and wooden cars. Tinker’s Toys sources 90% of our materials from recycled wood. We believe in clean play, so all of our paints are non-toxic.

Target audience

The target audience is parents of children ages 0-8, who would also be the group of people to most often use this site. They would visit this site to look for and purchase wooden toys for their children.

About

Tinker's Toys was founded in 2011 on the idea that children should have access to high-quality, sustainable toys that encourage creativity and learning. All of our products are made from sustainable materials and are designed to be both fun and educational. We strive to use 90% recycled wood and all of our paint is non-toxic for safer fun for your children.
