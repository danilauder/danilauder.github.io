Tinker's Toys was founded in 2011 on the idea that children should have access to high-quality, sustainable toys that encourage creativity and learning. All of our products are made from sustainable materials and are designed to be both fun and educational. We strive to use 90% recycled wood and all of our paint is non-toxic for safer fun for your children.

We believe that play is an essential part of a child's development, and our toys are designed to foster creativity, problem-solving skills, and social interaction. Our founder, Martha Smith, has dedicated her life to creating toys that inspire imagination and learning. She founded this company with the mission to provide children with toys that are both fun and educational.

We source our wood from sustainable forests here in the United States and use eco-friendly manufacturing processes to minimize our environmental impact. Over the years, we have partnered with various organizations to support environmental causes like reforestation and conservation. We understand the importance of preserving our planet for future generations, and that, as a company that uses wood, we must be careful with the footprint we leave behind.

Recently, we have partnered with international non-profit organizations to support their initiatives in promoting sustainable practices and environmental conservation. We hope to continue this important work and make a positive impact on our environment for years to come.
